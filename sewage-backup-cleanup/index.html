Sewage backup cleanup in Rosedale, NY handles waste water that has come up through a floor drain, toilet or tub. It is classed as Category 3 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which means it can carry bacteria and other contaminants and must be removed with protective equipment.

If you smell sewage, see dark water coming from a drain or hear gurgling in the toilets, keep people and pets out of the area and call (516) 845-9582. We tell you what to do before the crew arrives.

Sewage smell from the drain or water coming up the floor drain

Most people find this through symptoms: a rotten egg or sewer smell, a toilet that bubbles when the washer drains, or dark water rising in a basement floor drain after rain. These point to a blocked or overloaded line.

Do not flush toilets or run water while the line is backed up, because every gallon you add can come back into the house. Once the water is up, the cleanup should be done by trained crews.

Sewage backup emergency: safety first

Treat any sewage as hazardous. Do not try to clean it with household tools.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Keep children, pets and everyone else out of the affected area.
  2. Stop using water. Do not flush toilets, run the washer or take showers.
  3. Do not walk through the water. If you must, wear waterproof boots and gloves, and wash thoroughly afterward.
  4. If water is near the panel or outlets, call your electric utility or 911 before anything else.
  5. Report raw sewage backing up into your home to 311, then call (516) 845-9582.

The NYC Department of Environmental Protection says to report raw sewage backing up into a home to 311, and that it inspects within six hours of receiving the complaint. Give your contact information so DEP can follow up.

What the cleanup involves

Crews extract the water and remove porous materials that cannot be cleaned, including carpet, pad and soaked drywall. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected, and air scrubbers help control odor and airborne particles during the work.

Drying follows once the contamination is gone. Moisture readings confirm that walls and floors have reached dry standards before rebuilding starts.
