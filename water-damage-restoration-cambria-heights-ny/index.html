Housing from 1931: what an older attached home means for water

In 2022 the city's Landmarks Preservation Commission designated two Cambria Heights historic districts, the 222nd Street and 227th Street districts, covering 96 Tudor-style rowhouses built in 1931. Many neighboring blocks share the same era and style. That age matters because plumbing, drain lines and roofs of that period may have been repaired or replaced over the decades, and a plumber can tell you what your house has.

Attached houses share party walls. A burst line in one house can drip through the wall into the neighbor's, so call your neighbor and your building's insurer quickly, and let our crew check the shared wall when we dry yours. Water in a party wall often needs both sides opened to dry fully.

When water is spreading in Cambria Heights

If water is moving through floors or walls, do not wait to see whether it stops.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Find the main valve, often near the meter, and turn it off if a pipe is the source.
  2. Run the lowest cold tap for a moment to relieve pressure in the lines.
  3. Stay away from wet outlets and the panel. If water reaches them, phone your electric utility or 911 before you phone us.
  4. Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet area and photograph them.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582 and tell us whether the wall or ceiling is shared with a neighbor.

Historic blocks and repairs: ask before exterior work

On blocks inside the two designated historic districts, the Landmarks Preservation Commission has a say in exterior changes. Interior water damage drying is a different matter, but a roof, window or brick repair on the outside may need LPC approval, so ask the LPC before ordering exterior work after a storm or leak.

For anything outside those districts, the NYC Department of Buildings states that plastering and painting need no permit and that ordinary plumbing repair must be done by a licensed master plumber. Ask before starting work, because exempt work still has to follow other rules.

Sewer lines and drains in Cambria Heights

DEP states that property owners own and must maintain the sewer service line from the house to the city main, and that raw sewage backing up into a home should be reported to 311, with DEP inspecting within six hours of the complaint. Keep your 311 tracking number.

If a backup follows heavy rain, tell 311 so DEP can tell a city system problem from a private line failure. Our sewage backup page explains the cleanup. Neighbors in Lawrence are in Nassau County and follow a different permit process.
