A boulevard with a flood record and a bluebelt next door

A bluebelt is a planned stormwater area, not a promise that no water will reach a house. For a homeowner, the useful step is to check the FEMA flood map for your address and to keep sump pumps, floor drains and downspouts working.

When water is rising in a basement

Basements fill fast and the danger is electrical. Act in this order.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Do not go down the stairs if the water is near outlets, a furnace or the panel.
  2. If the water is only on the floor and the panel is dry, turn off the main water valve to rule out a burst line.
  3. Unplug nothing that stands in water. Call your electric utility if the meter or panel is wet.
  4. Move boxes and papers up from the lowest shelves once the room is safe.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582 and tell us whether the water is clear, gray or dark and smelly.

Tracing the name: Foster's Meadow

This area was once called Foster's Meadow, a colonial name tied to fresh water, and Brookville Boulevard was Old Foster's Meadow Road until the 1920s. Most of Rosedale was built up as suburban housing in later decades, with much of the architecture dating from the 1950s and 1960s.

We mention the history only because older houses may have repaired or replaced plumbing, and a licensed plumber can tell you what yours has. We do not assume that any house here is more likely to leak.

Sewer backups and the NYC rules that apply

The owner is responsible for the sewer pipe that runs from the house to the city main, and DEP asks residents to report sewage backing up into a home through 311. Write down the complaint number you are given, because the agency uses it to follow up.

Sewage is never a do-it-yourself drying job. Our sewage backup page explains protective cleanup, and the water extraction page explains how standing water is removed before drying starts. Professional drying follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and you may ask any company whether its crews work to it.

Common questions

Where is Brookville in relation to Rosedale?

Brookville Park is on Rosedale's western border, next to Springfield Gardens, and Brookville Boulevard runs through the area. Part of the neighborhood sits along the line with Nassau County, so permit and agency questions can differ by block.

Did Brookville Boulevard flood in past storms?

The National Weather Service lists flooding on Brookville Boulevard from 149th Street to Rockaway Boulevard when the Inwood gauge reached 8.8 feet on June 4, 2012. That is a road record, so it does not tell you about any one home.

Is a sump pump enough for heavy rain?

A pump helps only while it has power and a clear discharge line. Test it before a storm, know where it drains, and call a plumber if it runs without stopping. We cannot promise that any system will stop water from entering.

What should I do first if a basement floods?

Stay out of the water if any outlet, cord or appliance may be live. Call your electric utility if the panel or meter is wet, photograph the damage from a safe spot and then call us.
