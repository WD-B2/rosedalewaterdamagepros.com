Burst pipe water damage in Rosedale, NY starts with a pressurized supply line that has split, popped a fitting or pulled out of a joint. Household supply lines typically run at 40 to 80 psi, so a clean break can push out hundreds of gallons in an hour before anyone is home to see it.

Your first job is the main shutoff valve, and ours is everything after it. Call (516) 845-9582 at any hour and we start with a plan for extraction, drying and the paperwork your insurer will ask for.

Burst pipe emergency: what to do before the crew arrives

Do not wait to see if the leak slows down. A pressurized line keeps running until someone closes a valve.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Close the main water valve, usually where the line enters the house near the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Open a cold faucet on the lowest floor to drain the remaining water from the pipes.
  3. If water is dripping through a light fixture or ceiling, keep clear and do not touch switches. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it on dry ground.
  4. Move rugs, electronics and paper out of the wet zone and photograph everything before you move it.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582. Tell us the room, the ceiling or wall that is wet and how long the water ran.

Plumbing repairs inside a New York City home must be done by a licensed master plumber, according to the NYC Department of Buildings. We handle the water damage side and can work around your plumber's schedule.

Why burst pipes cause more damage than slow leaks

A burst line fails in minutes, not months. Water follows framing, wicks up drywall and drips through floors into the room below, which is why a kitchen supply line can end up damaging a ceiling and a basement ceiling in the same hour.

Unlike a slow drip, the water is usually clean at the start. That window matters. Clean water is classed as Category 1 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and carpet, pad and drywall can often be dried instead of replaced when drying begins quickly. After roughly 48 hours it degrades toward Category 2 and the options shrink.

What crews do after the pipe is isolated

Once the line is shut off, the work follows a set order so nothing is missed.

  • Extract standing water and pull water out of carpet and pad.
  • Map moisture with meters and a thermal camera, including the wall behind the break and the ceiling below.
  • Open walls or ceilings where wet insulation traps water against framing.
  • Set air movers and dehumidifiers and log daily readings until each material meets its drying goal.
  • Document the loss with photos and a written scope you can send your insurer.

Related work often follows. If the water reached cabinets, see kitchen water damage restoration, and if drywall needs rebuilding, wall and drywall repair covers it.

Burst pipe notes for Rosedale homeowners

NYC emergency repairs can start before a permit is issued if a licensed contractor files an Emergency Work Notification within two business days of finishing, per the Department of Buildings. Ask your plumber whether that applies to your repair.

Rosedale and nearby Cambria Heights homes get water from the NYC Department of Environmental Protection. If you suspect a leak between the street and your meter, call 311 and DEP, and call us if water has entered the house.

Common questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Rosedale?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call and describe the leak, and we tell you what we can do the same day.

Should I turn off the electricity?

Only if the panel is dry and you can reach it standing on dry ground. If water is near the panel or running down a wall with outlets, stay out and call your electric utility or 911.

Is a burst pipe covered by homeowners insurance?

Sudden and accidental pipe breaks are often covered, but coverage depends on your policy and cause. File promptly, photograph the damage before cleanup and ask your insurer what is covered.
