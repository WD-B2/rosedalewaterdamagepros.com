Flood damage cleanup in Rosedale, NY deals with water that came from outside, from storm drains, creeks or tidal surges, not from a pipe inside the house. That water carries soil, sewage and chemicals, and it is classed as Category 3 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

This page covers river, groundwater and surface flooding. If your water came from a pipe, our burst pipe page fits better, and for wind-driven rain through a roof or window see storm water damage cleanup.

Flood emergency: what to do before cleanup starts

Flood water is a health and electrical hazard from the first minute. Treat it as unsafe until professionals say otherwise.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Stay out of standing water. It may be electrically charged or contaminated.
  2. If water is near the panel or outlets, do not enter. Call your electric utility or 911 before calling us.
  3. If you are told to evacuate, leave. Property is replaceable.
  4. Photograph high-water marks and damaged belongings from a safe spot.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582 and describe the depth, how it entered and which floors are wet.

We do not promise an arrival time during area-wide flooding, because roads and crews are limited. We do explain what we can do the same day and give you a written scope before work starts.

What makes flood water different

Category 3 water carries bacteria and debris from storm drains, soil and sometimes sewage. Porous materials such as carpet, pad, insulation and unsealed drywall that stayed in it are usually removed instead of dried. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected.

Crews wear protective gear and use extraction pumps, then set air movers and dehumidifiers once contaminated materials are out. Moisture readings confirm each cavity is dry before repairs begin.

Flood notes for Rosedale

Rosedale sits next to Jamaica Bay. The National Weather Service New York office documents tidal flooding at Hungry Harbor Road and Hook Creek Boulevard in Rosedale, and widespread flooding at Meadowmere during Hurricane Sandy in 2012, when the Jamaica Bay tide reached 14.1 feet above mean lower low water. If you live near those streets, tidal flooding can arrive with little rain.

Check the FEMA flood map and the NYC Flood Hazard Mapper for your address, and ask your insurer about a separate flood policy. New York's Department of Financial Services states that the typical homeowners policy does not cover flood damage, and the Village of Lawrence adds that flood policies carry a 30-day waiting period. Common questions

Do you handle flood emergencies at night or on weekends in Rosedale?

Yes. We answer 24 hours a day, including holidays and during storms. Call, describe the water and we tell you what is possible that day.

Is flood water covered by homeowners insurance?

Usually not. The New York Department of Financial Services notes that the typical policy does not cover flood, so ask about a separate flood policy or an endorsement.

Can wet drywall be saved after a flood?

If it was in contaminated water, it is usually cut out. Clean water from a pipe is a different case, and drywall can sometimes be dried.

Is it safe to stay in the house?

Not while there is standing water, electrical risk or sewage. Follow evacuation guidance and ask professionals to check the home first.
