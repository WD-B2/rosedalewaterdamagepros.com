What the Weather Service records for Meadowmere

The National Weather Service ties coastal flood impacts in Rosedale to readings on the Inwood tide gauge. In Hurricane Sandy in 2012 the gauge reached 14.1 feet, and the service records major residential damage at Meadowmere.

The lesson for a homeowner is practical: tide and storm surge reach this edge of Queens, so keep a plan for contaminated water.

If tidal or storm water is in the house

Do not wade through it if any outlet, cord or appliance may be live. Water that came from outside should be treated as unsafe.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Keep people and pets out of the water. Children should not play in it.
  2. Do not touch the breaker panel if you would be standing in water. Call your electric utility or 911 if the panel or meter is wet.
  3. Do not run a household vacuum or fans in standing flood water.
  4. Take photos of the waterline on the walls from a safe spot.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582 and tell us how deep the water was and whether it came from the street or a drain.

Drainage work along Hook Creek

The city announced a $51.8 million drainage project in 2024 that reaches this part of Rosedale. Storm sewers, catch basins and underground storm chambers are going in across 36 blocks of the Francis Lewis, Hook Creek and Hungry Harbor corridors, and completion is expected in winter 2026.

Better street drainage can reduce ponding, but it does not protect a basement, a water heater or a washing machine hose. Test your sump pump if you have one, clear your floor drain and keep low rooms free of stored paper.

Contaminated water and drying in a small, low neighborhood

Water that has touched a street, a creek or a sewer is graded as the most contaminated category. Porous items such as carpet, pad and wet drywall usually have to come out, because they cannot be made safe by drying alone. Crews then extract, clean, and dry the structure to written goals.

Hard surfaces and framing can often be cleaned and dried in place. Our sewage backup page explains how contaminated cleanup differs from a clean pipe leak, and our structural drying page explains the equipment.

Common questions

Is Meadowmere part of Rosedale?

Yes. Meadowmere is a small community inside Rosedale, Queens, and Queens Community Board 13 lists it among its communities. It shares Rosedale ZIP code 11422 in published listings, so confirm the ZIP on your own mail.

Does tidal water count as flood damage?

Yes. Water that comes in from a creek, a bay or the street is flood water, and it is usually treated as contaminated. It needs protective cleanup, and it is not a job for household fans or a store vacuum.

Will homeowners insurance pay for storm flooding?

Often not. Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood, and flood cover is a separate policy such as one under the National Flood Insurance Program. Read your policy and ask your insurer, and photograph the loss before cleanup.

How do I find the flood zone for my address?

Use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and type in your address. We have not verified a flood zone for Meadowmere as a whole, and zones can differ from one street to the next.
