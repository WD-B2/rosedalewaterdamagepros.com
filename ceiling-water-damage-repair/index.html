Ceiling water damage repair in Rosedale, NY covers stains, sagging drywall and active drips, whatever the source. The cause can be a roof leak, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or an attic HVAC drain, and the repair starts by finding which.

A ceiling that bulges with water can fail suddenly, and wet drywall can weigh far more than dry. Keep people out from under it and call the 24/7 line. Roof leaks have no separate page here, so the roof-leak section below is part of this service.

When a ceiling is an emergency

A ceiling is an emergency when it is dripping, sagging, bulging or has any water near a light fixture.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Move people and pets out of the room below the damage.
  2. Do not poke a bulge unless you can place a bucket underneath and stay clear of the area it will fall into.
  3. Do not turn on a light fixture or fan in the wet area. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it on dry ground.
  4. Move furniture and electronics out from under the stain and protect floors with plastic.
  5. Photograph the ceiling, then call (516) 845-9582.

If the water is from an upstairs fixture, shut off the main valve.

What to do when a ceiling is wet

A wet ceiling is a falling and shock risk, so protect people first.

  • Stay out from under a sagging or bulging ceiling.
  • Place a bucket under drips and move furniture away.
  • Turn off lights and fixtures on that circuit if water is near them.
  • Poke a small hole only if a trained person says to, because a bulge can hold a lot of water.
  • Photograph the stain and note when it first appeared.

Finding the real source

The stain is rarely directly below the problem. Water travels along joists and pipes before it drops. A stain in a living room can come from a bathroom above and to one side, and a stain near an exterior wall can come from the roof edge.

Moisture meters and thermal imaging show where the wet area starts and stops. The crew also checks the attic, the room above and the framing for staining that tells whether the leak is new or old.

Roof leak water damage: what is inside the ceiling

A roof leak soaks insulation, then drywall, and the insulation holds water against the ceiling long after rain stops. This section is part of the ceiling service because the damage you see inside is the same whatever the source.

Roof repair itself belongs to a roofer, and non-structural roof repair in New York City does not require a permit, according to the NYC Department of Buildings, though the work still has to follow other rules. We handle the interior: wet insulation removal, drywall cutting, drying and rebuilding. The two trades coordinate so the leak is closed before ceilings are finished.

Texture, paint and finish after the repair

Ceiling repairs show more than wall repairs because light rakes across them. Matching a textured or popcorn ceiling is hard, and a smooth ceiling needs a full coat of primer and paint to avoid a visible patch.

Plan for the finish to cover a full ceiling section rather than a spot. We scope repairs and paint together so the room looks uniform, and we note it in the written scope before work begins.

Can I paint over a ceiling stain?

Not until the source is fixed and the area is dry. Painting over a damp ceiling hides the problem and the stain usually returns.

How do I know if the ceiling will collapse?

A bulge, sag or steady drip means it can fail. Keep clear and call.
