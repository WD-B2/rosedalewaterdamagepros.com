Basement water damage cleanup in Rosedale, NY covers the below-grade room: sump pump failures, foundation seepage, floor drain backups and water that comes down from above. Basements collect water because they sit lowest, and finished basements hide it behind drywall and carpet.

Restoration is not waterproofing. We clean and dry the space, and a waterproofing contractor handles the cause if it is a foundation problem. Call (516) 845-9582 and we start with extraction.

When a basement is an emergency

Standing water in a basement can reach panels, furnaces and water heaters, so treat it as an emergency until it is cleared.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Do not go down if water is near outlets or the panel. Call your electric utility or 911.
  2. If the water is rising from a drain, stay out and treat it as Category 3.
  3. Stop any running source and close the main valve if a pipe is involved.
  4. Move belongings upstairs if you can do so safely from dry stairs.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582 and tell us the depth and the finishes in the room.

Never use a gas-powered pump or generator in the basement. Exhaust gases are deadly.

Why basement water behaves differently

Water collects at the lowest point and has nowhere to go. A sump pump that fails, a power outage or a blocked drain can fill a basement quickly, and groundwater can push through floor cracks and wall seams.

Stored boxes, finished walls and carpet soak up water. Outdoor water is Category 3, which changes what can be saved.

Protecting belongings in a wet basement

Basements hold the things people store and rarely check: documents, photo albums, holiday decorations and furniture. Cardboard boxes at floor level wick water upward, and paper items can be damaged within a day.

Lift what you can safely reach onto shelves or higher floors, and move photographs and papers out of wet boxes into dry containers. Our crews can pack out items for cleaning where needed, and we list what was moved so you can account for it later.

Mechanical equipment in a wet basement

Furnaces, boilers, water heaters and electrical panels often sit in the basement and need special care.

Electrical panel

If water is near the panel, do not touch it. Call your electric utility or 911.

Furnace and boiler

Wet controls and burners need inspection by a licensed technician before use.

Water heater

Check for a gas smell and call the gas utility if you notice one.

Sump pump

A failed pump may need replacing, so call a plumber once the area is safe.

Common questions

Do you handle basement water emergencies at night or on weekends in Rosedale?

Yes. We answer 24/7, so a basement that floods at 2 a.m. gets the same call as one that floods at noon. Describe the depth and what is stored down there, and we tell you what to do while help is arranged.
