How coverage works

Our primary ZIP is 11422 in Rosedale. We also cover 11411 for Cambria Heights and 11559 for Lawrence. Rosedale and Cambria Heights are neighborhoods of Queens (NYC), while Lawrence is a village in Nassau County, so permit and agency questions differ across the line.

If you are in Queens, the NYC Department of Buildings and the Department of Environmental Protection apply. If you are in Lawrence, the Village Building Department and Nassau County agencies apply. Our location pages explain the difference in more detail.

Rosedale streets and flood history

The National Weather Service New York office lists coastal flood impacts for Rosedale. At 10.2 feet at the Inwood gauge on December 23, 2022, Hungry Harbor Road flooded from 148th Avenue southward. At 9.1 feet on February 8, 2016, Hungry Harbor Road and Hook Creek Boulevard flooded. At 8.8 feet on June 4, 2012, Brookville Boulevard flooded from 149th Street to Rockaway Boulevard.

During Hurricane Sandy in 2012, the gauge reached 14.1 feet and the same office records major residential damage at Meadowmere. If you live near these streets, check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your city building department about flood protection for your block.

Drainage work in Rosedale

A $51.8 million drainage and sewer project in Rosedale was announced in 2024 by the city's DEP and DDC. It adds storm sewers, catch basins and underground storm chambers, and it also covers sanitary sewer and water main work across 36 blocks of the Francis Lewis, Hook Creek and Hungry Harbor corridors. The agencies expect completion in winter 2026.

Better drainage can reduce street flooding, but private plumbing, roofs and basements still need care. Clear your drains, test your sump pump and keep an eye on low rooms.

Cambria Heights and Lawrence

Cambria Heights has two Landmarks Preservation Commission historic districts, on 222nd and 227th Streets, built around Tudor style rowhouses. Exterior work there can involve extra review, so ask before you repair the outside of a home. See Cambria Heights water damage restoration.

Lawrence sits along Bannister Creek, Reynolds Channel and Brosewere Bay, and the Village publishes flood information through its Building Department. See Lawrence water damage restoration.

What affects how soon we can respond

Response depends on the time of day, traffic on the Belt Parkway and Sunrise Highway corridors, tides, storms and crew availability. Severe weather can mean many calls at once, and we say so honestly instead of promising a time we cannot keep.

When you call, we tell you what to do while help is on the way. Turning off water at the main valve, staying out of rooms with water near outlets and moving valuables up are all steps that can reduce damage before a crew arrives.

Choosing the right first step

If the water is clean and from a pipe, the first step is to stop it and call. If the water came from a sewer backup or a flood, treat it as contaminated, avoid contact and call. If the damage is old and the area just smells musty, find the damp material and dry it, and dry it before you cover or paint anything.

Whichever applies, document the damage with photos before cleanup. That helps if you file a claim with your insurer.
