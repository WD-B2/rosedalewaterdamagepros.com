Frozen pipe water damage in Rosedale, NY usually appears at the thaw, not during the freeze. Ice expands inside a pipe, a hairline split opens, and the water only runs once the ice melts and pressure returns. That is when a quiet wall becomes a wet one.

If a pipe has already split, close the main valve and call the 24/7 line. If the pipe is only frozen, a few careful steps can sometimes prevent the split, and we explain them below.

How a frozen pipe becomes a flood

Water expands about 9 percent as it turns to ice. The worst split often forms not at the ice itself but between the ice plug and a closed faucet, where trapped pressure has nowhere to go. The pipe looks fine until the plug melts, and then it sprays.

Pipes in exterior walls, garages, unheated basements and crawl spaces freeze first. A faucet that gives only a trickle on a hard-freeze morning is the classic warning.

Frozen pipe emergency: what to do right now

Whether the line is merely frozen or has already split, the first moves are the same.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Close the main water valve before you thaw anything.
  2. Open the affected faucet so melting water has somewhere to go.
  3. Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or warm towels. Never use an open flame or a torch.
  4. If you see a split, spray or a wet wall, leave the valve closed and call (516) 845-9582.
  5. Keep cabinet doors open under sinks on exterior walls to let warm air in.

If water is already near outlets or a panel, stay out of the room and call your electric utility or 911 before calling us.

What recovery looks like

Crews extract the water, then find every wet cavity. A frozen exterior wall may hold wet insulation against cold sheathing, which dries slowly and stays damp for weeks unless it is opened. Moisture mapping confirms where the water went and sets drying goals for each material.

Drying equipment runs until readings come down to the dry standard for that material. If drywall has been soaked, it can be cut out in sections and rebuilt, and our wall and drywall water damage repair page explains what that involves. For deeper wet floors, see structural drying.

Common questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Rosedale?

Yes. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, including holidays. Cold snaps are exactly when we expect calls at odd hours.

Should I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Only with gentle warmth and with the main valve closed. Never use a torch or open flame, and call a plumber if the pipe looks bulged or cracked.

How do I know if a pipe split inside the wall?

Wet drywall, a stained ceiling below, a sudden drop in water pressure or the sound of running water with every fixture off are all warning signs.

Can you repair the pipe?

A New York City plumbing repair requires a licensed master plumber. We handle extraction, drying and restoration around that repair.
