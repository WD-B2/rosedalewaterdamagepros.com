If storm water has entered a room

Water that follows heavy rain may carry sewage or street runoff. Treat it as unsafe until it is cleaned.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Keep children and pets out of the room and away from the water.
  2. Leave the panel alone if the floor is wet. If the meter or panel is wet, call your electric utility or 911.
  3. Do not use a household vacuum to pick up the water.
  4. Photograph the wet items and how high the water reached.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582 and tell us how deep the water was and how long it stayed.

Heavy rain and ponding on Springfield Boulevard

Water that stands in the street for days is a good reason to watch low rooms. Check the bottom of exterior doors, window wells and the floor drain after each heavy rain, and look for damp baseboards that appear a day or two later.

Report street flooding to 311 so the city has a record. For water inside the house, tell us what you can see and we will suggest the next step. Ask your city building department before you start repairs that may need a permit.

From extraction to drying in a low-rise home

In a low-rise home, water usually reaches a basement or a ground floor first. Crews extract standing water, remove materials that cannot be dried, clean the structure and set air movers and dehumidifiers. Daily moisture readings show when the structure reaches its drying goal.

Common questions

Is Springfield Gardens inside Rosedale?

No. It is a separate Queens community that borders Rosedale, and Rosedale and Laurelton meet it on the east. We take calls from both and the NYC rules are the same on either side of the line.

Which ZIP codes does Springfield Gardens use?

Published sources list 11434 for the western part and 11413 for the eastern part. Check the ZIP on your own mail, and tell us your street when you call so we can confirm what we can do.

Has the city worked on flooding here?

Yes. The city's 2018 release on southeast Queens lists Springfield Lake among its bluebelt stormwater areas and Springfield Boulevard among its large trunk sewer routes. Street projects do not stop water from entering a private basement.

Does insurance cover storm flooding?

Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood. Flood cover is a separate policy, and sudden pipe damage is treated differently. Ask your insurer, check your policy and photograph the loss before cleanup.
