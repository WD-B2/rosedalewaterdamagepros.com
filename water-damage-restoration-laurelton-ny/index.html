Tudor-style blocks and how water moves through them

Laurelton was laid out by developer Dean Alvord, who modeled it on an English village. Reporting describes Tudor-style houses, some attached and some detached, with landscaped center malls on a few streets. Near Springfield Gardens there are more multi-family buildings.

In attached houses, water from a pipe or roof can cross a party wall. In detached houses, it usually stays in one structure but can travel between floors. Either way, the first step is to find and stop the source.

When a pipe or roof is dripping inside

Shut the water off first. A steady drip can become a flood in the time it takes to find a plumber.

Do this first, in this order

  1. Turn off the fixture valve or the main valve, often near the meter.
  2. Open a low cold tap to relieve pressure after the main is off.
  3. If a ceiling is bulging, keep away from it and do not poke it open.
  4. Switch off power only if you can reach the breaker without standing in water.
  5. Call (516) 845-9582 and say whether a neighbor shares the wall or ceiling.

Merrick Boulevard businesses and mixed buildings

Merrick Boulevard is the commercial spine of Laurelton, so a building may combine a shop or office with homes. Where that is the case, water from an upstairs unit can reach the one below, and the owner, the tenant and the insurer may all need a record of what happened.

We handle commercial water damage as well as homes. If a lease or a condo rule applies, ask the landlord or the managing agent who is responsible before ordering repairs.

Sewer lines and rules in New York City

According to DEP, the pipe between the house and the city main belongs to the owner. If sewage backs up, call 311 and keep the tracking number it gives you. The city's southeast Queens drainage program names Laurelton among its neighborhoods, but street work does not repair a private line.

For ordinary repairs, ask the NYC Department of Buildings what needs a permit. Our sewage backup page explains protective cleanup, and drying work should follow the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

Common questions

What ZIP codes does Laurelton use?

Published sources list 11413 and 11422 for Laurelton. Mail and delivery boundaries do not always match neighborhood boundaries, so check your own mail and tell us your street when you call.

What kind of homes are in Laurelton?

News reports describe Tudor-style homes, both attached and detached, with large individually designed houses south of Merrick Boulevard and attached rowhouses on some streets north of it. We do not assume what is inside yours, so a plumber should check.

Does shared wall water need both sides opened?

Often, yes. Water in an attached house can pass into a party wall and the neighbor's side, so the wall may need to be checked and dried on both sides. Tell your neighbor and your insurer quickly.

Does Laurelton have a historic district?

We have not verified a Laurelton historic district, so we make no claim about one. Ask the Landmarks Preservation Commission before ordering exterior repairs if your block is unclear.
