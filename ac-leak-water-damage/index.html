AC leak water damage repair in Rosedale, NY covers the ceilings, walls and floors soaked by a clogged condensate line, a cracked drain pan or a frozen evaporator coil. Air conditioners remove gallons of water from humid air each day, and when the drain path fails, that water ends up in your building instead of outside.

Because the leak is often slow, the damage tends to be hidden. A faint ceiling stain, a musty smell near a closet air handler or a wet drain pan are the early signs. Call (516) 845-9582 for an assessment and we dry and repair what the drip has reached.

Why air conditioners leak inside the building

Cooling coils sweat. The water drips into a pan and drains through a condensate line, usually a small PVC pipe. Algae, dust and slime clog that pipe over a season, and once it backs up, the pan overflows or the line cracks at a joint.

A frozen coil causes the same result later. Ice builds up on the coil when airflow is low or refrigerant is off, and when it melts it can overflow the pan. In attics and closets, the water soaks insulation and drywall before anyone sees it.

What to do before the crew arrives

Start with the system itself, then the water.

  • Turn the thermostat to off so the system stops making condensate.
  • Shut off power to the air handler at the breaker if water is near it.
  • Do not step in standing water near the unit or outlets.
  • Move rugs and stored items out of the closet or attic floor.
  • Photograph the pan, the drain line and the wet area.

How the repair works

Crews start by shutting the system off and finding moisture with a meter and thermal camera. Insulation that has absorbed water is removed, because it holds moisture against drywall for weeks. Ceiling or wall sections that have swollen or softened are cut out in clean lines so the cavity can dry.

Air movers and a dehumidifier run until readings return to dry standards, and then the drywall, paint or flooring are rebuilt. Your HVAC technician should clear or replace the drain line during the same visit so the leak does not repeat.

Preventing a repeat

Have an HVAC technician clean the condensate line each season, check the pan and install a float switch that shuts the system off if the pan fills. Replace the filter on schedule, since a dirty filter starves the coil of air and invites ice. A pan under an attic air handler is cheap protection for the ceiling below it.

Common questions

How do I know if my AC is leaking inside the wall or ceiling?

Look for a stain under the air handler, a musty smell, peeling paint near a vent or a wet pan. A moisture meter and thermal camera can confirm what you cannot see.

Can a small AC leak cause real damage?

Yes. A slow drip that runs all summer soaks insulation and drywall and can rot framing.

Do you repair the AC unit?

No. An HVAC technician repairs the unit. We dry and repair the building.

Is AC leak damage covered by insurance?

Sudden overflow may be covered, but gradual leaks are often excluded. Ask your insurer.
